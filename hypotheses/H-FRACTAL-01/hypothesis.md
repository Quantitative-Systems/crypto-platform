# H-FRACTAL-01 — Cross-Scale Mechanism Transfer

## Status
UNDER_RESEARCH

## Research question
If a mechanism shows DEV evidence at one timeframe scale, does it retain measurable economic behavior at other scales/assets?

## Null hypothesis
Performance at one scale provides no reliable evidence of positive expectancy at another scale/asset.

## Falsification criteria
DEV expectancy <= 0.0R and PF <= 1.0 at every other tested scale; no cross-scale correlation above chance.

## Mechanism summary
A valid market mechanism may exhibit partial transfer across timeframe scales/assets; must be demonstrated empirically.

## Decision
Infrastructure registered. DO NOT TEST yet.
