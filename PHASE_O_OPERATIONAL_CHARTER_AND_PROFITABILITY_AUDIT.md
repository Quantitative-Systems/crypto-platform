## 2. THE MULTI-STAGE DEGRADATION PIPELINE

The difference between research expectancy and paper realized outcome is not merely execution drag. Paper trading dynamically alters **which trades occur**. The engine decomposes performance across four distinct degradation stages:

$$\boxed{ \text{Research Signal} \xrightarrow{\text{Stage 1}} \text{Opportunity Selection} \xrightarrow{\text{Stage 2}} \text{Execution} \xrightarrow{\text{Stage 3}} \text{Carry \& Financing} \xrightarrow{\text{Stage 4}} \text{Realized Outcome} }$$

1. **Alpha Degradation**: $E[\text{Research Opportunity}] \longrightarrow E[\text{Paper Opportunity}]$.  
   Reflects shifts in price structure, order block respect, or regime changes in the live market.
2. **Opportunity Selection Degradation**: $N[\text{Research Opportunities}] \longrightarrow N[\text{Paper Executable Trades}]$.  
   Caused by spread filters, order rejections, stale quotes, liquidity constraints, and event freezes.
3. **Execution Degradation**: $P[\text{Theoretical}] \longrightarrow P[\text{Paper Fill}]$.  
   Caused by bid-ask spread crossing, gateway latency, and adverse market slippage.
4. **Carry & Financing Degradation**:  
   Caused by perpetual funding rate payments, exchange taker fees, and cross-currency conversion basis.
