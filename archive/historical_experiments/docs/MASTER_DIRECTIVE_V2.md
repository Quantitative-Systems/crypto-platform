# 49. OPERATING PRINCIPLE

QCP must become capable of answering this question continuously:

> **“Given everything QCP currently knows about the crypto market, its data, its research history, its portfolio, its risk, its execution environment and its economic evidence, what is the highest-value next action?”**

That action may be:

* research a new alpha
* acquire data
* repair infrastructure
* test a hypothesis
* falsify an alpha
* improve execution
* improve capacity estimation
* investigate a regime
* hedge
* reduce capital
* quarantine an alpha
* replace an alpha
* expand an asset universe
* add a venue
* improve monitoring
* or do nothing

Doing nothing is valid when evidence says nothing should change.

---

# 50. AUTONOMOUS DECISION RULE

Antigravity must NOT ask the human:

> “Which strategy should I build next?”

unless the decision genuinely requires a human authorization outside the system's authority.

Instead:

1. inspect the system;
2. identify the highest-value unresolved problem;
3. gather evidence;
4. choose the next action;
5. execute it;
6. test it;
7. record the result;
8. update the capability/economic state;
9. choose the next action;
10. repeat.

---

# 51. STOP CONDITIONS

Do not stop because:

* one strategy works;
* tests are green;
* a report looks impressive;
* a backtest is profitable;
* a candidate has high historical R;
* a subsystem was implemented.

Stop only when:

1. the current milestone is genuinely complete;
2. its evidence is recorded;
3. regression is passing;
4. repository state is clean;
5. the next milestone has been determined.

If the system reaches a true production-ready state, transition into continuous production + research mode rather than declaring the project finished.
