# STRATA — Product Roadmap

## Roadmap Overview
The STRATA roadmap transitions the platform through structured stages of development, formalization, paper deployment, and live capital authorization.

---

## Phase Milestones

### Phase 1: Research & Forensic Certification (Completed)
- **Phase P**: Quantitative discovery of multi-timeframe structural edge.
- **Phase Q / Q.1**: Forensic audit and validation of the continuous 7-timeframe fractal hierarchy across 5 overlapping triad sets.
- **Phase Q.2**: Incremental value attribution proving +0.3105R edge improvement ($\Delta \text{Expectancy} = +53.7\%$, Profit Factor: 2.35 → 4.92, Max Drawdown: 33.8R → 10.9R).
- **Certification**: Replay regression locks at 9,608 / 9,608 historical trades.

### Phase 2: Production Hardening & Zero-Capital Baseline (Completed)
- **Phase R**: Frozen trading core locked under SHA-256 contract.
- Build of CLI commands, test suites (192/192 baseline), watchdog loops, and paper execution simulation.
- Zero-capital gate enforced ($0.00 live limit).

### Phase 3: STRATA Product Application Build (Current Milestone — v2.0.0)
- **Protected KING Contract**: `execution/king/king_engine_contract.py`.
- **Multi-Tenancy & Authentication**: Scoped tenant contexts, encrypted session tokens, tenant isolation tests.
- **3 Strategy Domains**: Domain A (KING Core), Domain B (Built-in Library), Domain C (User Strategy Lab).
- **Strategy Lab Compiler**: Natural language conversion into verifiable `StrategySpecification`.
- **16-Stage Evidence Lifecycle**: State machine tracking `DRAFT` to `REPLACEMENT`.
- **Autonomous Agent**: Orchestrator for observation, style filtering, risk governance, and quarantine.
- **Broker & Account Center**: Binance, Bybit, MT5 adapter interfaces and Account Suitability Engine.
- **Web Platform**: Public marketing site (`/`) + Institutional Trading Terminal (`/app`).
- **Android Mobile**: Touch client with zero-secret storage and remote emergency halt.

### Phase 4: Forward Paper & Shadow Multi-Asset Validation (Next Quarter)
- Continuous shadow and paper streaming on Binance USDT-M and Bybit perpetuals.
- 90-day forward validation logging fill rates, execution latency, real-world slippage, and funding rate drag.
- Live performance drift evaluation via `ResearchEvolutionEngine`.

### Phase 5: Micro-Live Alpha Testing (Future Gated Release)
- Activation of Micro-Live execution tier ($10–$50 position sizes).
- Multi-party authorization required for capital limit adjustments.
- Real-time exchange position reconciliation and automated circuit breaker trips.

### Phase 6: Institutional Multi-Broker Controlled Live (Long-term)
- Expansion of institutional broker adapters (Kraken, Coinbase Advanced, OKX).
- Distributed high-availability clustering and automated failover gateways.
