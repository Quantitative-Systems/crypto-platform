Frequently Asked Questions

What is STRATA?
STRATA is a quantitative trading software platform that enables traders to research, test, validate, and automate systematic digital asset trading strategies.
Can I build my own strategy?
Yes. You can formulate trading rules manually or use the AI-assisted Strategy Lab to formalize your concepts into testable specifications.
Does STRATA guarantee profits?
No. Markets change and no strategy guarantees future returns. STRATA provides disciplined testing, risk governors, and validation tools.
Is live trading available right now?
No. Real capital remains locked at $0.00. The platform operates exclusively in zero-capital paper simulation and exchange demo/testnet modes during the launch period.
How much does STRATA cost?
STRATA is free during the entire first-year launch period with no platform subscription charges.
